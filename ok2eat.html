Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 4, 2026

ok2eat ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use the ok2eat mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide the app's core functionality:

  • Account Information: Your email address and password (stored securely and encrypted) when you create an account.
  • Fridge Data: Food items you add, including product names, categories, quantities, expiry dates, and barcodes.
  • Usage Data: Basic app usage patterns to help us improve the app experience.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not display advertisements.
  • We do not share your personal information with advertisers.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app's functionality.
  • To authenticate your account and keep your fridge data private.
  • To generate AI-powered recipe suggestions based on your fridge contents.
  • To send expiry notifications if you enable them.
  • To improve and develop new app features.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored securely using Supabase, a SOC 2 compliant cloud database provider. Your password is hashed and never stored in plain text. Your fridge data is protected by Row Level Security — only you can access your own items.

5. Third-Party Services

ok2eat uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase — secure user authentication and data storage
  • Open Food Facts — open-source food product database for barcode scanning
  • Anthropic Claude API — AI-powered recipe generation

These services have their own privacy policies and we encourage you to review them.

6. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

ok2eat is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.
