// What you get

Everything you need to waste nothing.

ok2eat is built around one question — what should you eat first, before it goes bad? Every feature below answers that, or makes the answer faster.

🔥
Eat Me First NEW
A new top-level tab that ranks your fridge by urgency. The thing closest to spoiling sits at the top. Tap any item to get three recipes that use it — plus the four next-most-urgent things alongside it — so dinner uses what's about to go bad instead of what you'd have to buy.
⏰
USDA-backed expiry dates
Per-item shelf life from the USDA FoodKeeper dataset plus FDA, FSIS, and Cooperative Extension publications — 950+ foods with their own typical fridge, freezer, and pantry windows. ok2eat stores both the printed date and the USDA window, so you stop throwing out yogurt that's still good.
📸
Receipt Scanning
Snap your grocery receipt and ok2eat fills your fridge for you in seconds. Every line gets smart expiry dates from FoodKeeper plus a smart container default — bread to pantry, ice cream to freezer, no manual sorting.
📊
Impact Dashboard NEW
Lifetime money saved, pounds of food rescued, CO₂ avoided. Live numbers, recalculated every time you mark something used. The average US household wastes $1,866 of food a year — yours doesn't have to.
🥗
Recipes that respect your diet NEW
Set dietary preferences (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, dairy-free, nut-free, low-carb, keto) and allergies (peanut, tree nut, shellfish, fish, egg, milk, soy, wheat, sesame) once. Every recipe respects them. Ingredient amounts scale to your household size.
📷
Barcode Scanner
For quick one-off additions. Scan a barcode and pull nutrition info, ingredients, and suggested expiry from a database of 3 million+ products.
🔔
Daily Digest
Get a morning summary by push notification, email, or both — your choice. See what's expiring, what's expired, and what to use first.
🛒
One-Tap Reorder
Tap any low-stock item and order it on Instacart, Amazon, or Walmart. Pre-filled search, your cart, no typing. Restock before you run out.
🤝
Shared Household
Add an item on your phone, your partner sees it on theirs. One fridge, one source of truth. Stop the "do we still have eggs?" texts.
📝
Shopping Lists, Together
Build separate lists for Costco, Trader Joe's, the weekday grab. Everyone in the house adds items in real time — and you can see who added what.
🥫
Cupboard + Fridge
Track pantry staples and fridge items side by side, in one place. Different shelf life, same simple interface.
🍽
Partial Use Tracking
Used half a gallon of milk? Added 2 tbsp of ketchup? Log partial usage in any unit and your inventory stays accurate.

// How it works

From "what do we want?" to "what do we have?"

Four steps. Less than a minute to set up. No complicated setup, no manual data entry.

// 40 seconds

The full tour

Add an item, open Eat Me First, tap a recipe, plan tomorrow's shop — start to finish in under a minute.

// Receipt scan

Photograph the receipt, skip the typing

After your grocery run, snap a picture of the receipt. ok2eat reads every line, categorizes each item, and sets a smart expiry date from USDA shelf-life data — so a 30-item haul lands in your fridge in seconds, not minutes. Available in the iOS app and at app.ok2eat.com once you've signed up.

01
Add what you bought
Scan a barcode for instant nutrition + expiry, type an item by name, or once you sign up — bulk-add by photographing your receipt. ok2eat sets smart shelf-life dates from USDA FoodKeeper data either way.
02
Get your morning digest
Push notification or email — your choice. See what's expiring, what's expired, and what to use first. No need to open the app to know what's at risk.
03
Open Eat Me First
Your fridge already sorted by urgency. Tap any item and get three recipe ideas that use it plus the four next-most-urgent things alongside it — respecting your dietary preferences and scaled to your household size.
04
Reorder in one tap
Items down to your last unit surface automatically. Tap once to send your shopping list to Instacart, Amazon, or Walmart — your cart is pre-filled before you arrive.

Common questions

  • Forgot your password? Tap "Forgot password" on the login screen — we'll email you a reset link.
  • Receipt scan not working? Make sure the receipt is well-lit and lying flat. Faded or crumpled receipts can be tricky for the scanner.
  • An item got the wrong expiry date? Tap the item to edit it. We'd also love a quick note at support@ so we can improve the model.
  • Want to delete your account? Email support@ok2eat.com from the address tied to your account and we'll remove it within 7 days.

Privacy or security questions?

For privacy questions, email privacy@ok2eat.com or read our Privacy Policy. For security researchers, our disclosure contact is in security.txt.
