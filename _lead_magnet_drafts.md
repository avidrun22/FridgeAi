## My recommendation

Start with **Variant A** for the first post. Reasons:

1. The founder voice does double duty — it builds the brand alongside the lead magnet
2. The 3 specific stats in line 3 give scrollers something to chew on even if they don't comment
3. It's the lowest-risk hook — won't generate angry-replier traffic that drags engagement
4. Matches the existing image perfectly, so it can ship today

If Variant A hits well (≥30 DMs in 48h), rotate to **Variant B** or **D** the following week. Save **Variant E** (contrarian) for when you're more comfortable with replies in your mentions.

## Posting time

Best windows for the food/finance niche on X:
- **Tue/Wed/Thu, 8-10am ET** — peak engagement for "look this up" content (people are at desks with coffee)
- **Sun, 6-8pm ET** — Sunday meal-prep mindset, people are planning the week

Avoid Friday afternoon and Saturday morning (lower engagement across all niches).
