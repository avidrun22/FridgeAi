## Caption

**Open with a bold line. Tease the breakdown. CTA to the blog. Tap "more" to keep reading.**

```
Fresh vegetables are up 44% on an annualized basis.
Your fridge throws out 30% of what you buy.

Those two numbers don't appear next to each other anywhere
except your bank statement.

April's CPI showed groceries jumped 0.7% in a single month —
the biggest one-month rise in almost four years. The headline
year-over-year number is 2.9%. The within-category numbers
(produce, coffee, beef) are uglier.

But here's what nobody is putting next to the inflation print:
the average household already throws away ~$1,866 of groceries
a year. That number doesn't fall when prices rise. It only
silently costs more.

So the real cost increase, for most households, isn't 2.9%.
It's compounded by what's already going to the trash.

Control what is in your power. Make better use of what you already have.

→ Full breakdown in the link in bio — ok2eat.com/blog

ok2eat is free on iPhone + web.
Less waste, more savings.
```

**Char count:** ~880. Well inside IG's 2,200 limit. First line shows above the "...more" fold — that's the hook.

## Posting workflow

1. **Schedule for Thursday 2026-05-14, ~9am Pacific.** Same morning the blog post goes live. News cycle is freshest, IG engagement peaks early.
2. **Update bio link** to point to `https://ok2eat.com/blog/april-grocery-inflation.html` (or a Linktree slot if you use one). Critical — the "link in bio" CTA fails if it lands on the home page.
3. **Cross-post to Facebook** via Meta Business Suite while you're at it.
4. **Story drop ~2 hours after feed post.** Don't post both simultaneously — Stories with link stickers convert ~3x better when they reference an already-live feed post.
5. **Engage in the comments for the first 2 hours.** IG rewards reply velocity. Real people answering questions outperforms anything else for reach.
