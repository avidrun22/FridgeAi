## Posting order / cadence suggestion

1. **Monday AM (8am PT)** — X thread + Threads chain (queue both via Typefully so they fire ~simultaneously; Threads cross-post is fine but post natively for engagement)
2. **Monday lunch (12pm PT)** — LinkedIn post
3. **Monday evening (6pm PT)** — Meta cross-post (IG + FB via Meta Business Suite)

Why staggered: LinkedIn engages mid-day, X/Threads engage AM, IG/FB engage
evening. Spreads the impressions instead of stacking them.

If you only have time for one channel: **X thread + cross-post to Threads**
is the highest signal-to-effort ratio for this audience.
